Project: Why are crypto deposits delayed?

User:
An operations analyst at a crypto platform.

Problem:
A customer has sent crypto, but their account balance has not updated.

Business question:
Where do deposits get delayed, and what should the team investigate first?

Deposit stages:
1. The customer initiates a transfer.
2. The blockchain records and confirms it.
3. The platform credits the customer's account.

Important distinction:
Blockchain confirmation and account credit are separate events.

Data:
I will start with five fictional deposits.
These records will not represent any real company's performance.
