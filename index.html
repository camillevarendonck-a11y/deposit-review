Required CSV format

Use exactly these five column names, in any order:

deposit_id
amount_usdc
initiated_at
confirmed_at
credited_at

Amounts: digits 0–9 with an optional decimal point, such as 1000.50. Dates must include a time: 2026-11-10T10:00:00. Blank confirmation and credit fields mean missing evidence. Times without an offset are treated as UTC.

    What these results establish

    These are record-based triage signals. Missing evidence does not prove an event never happened, establish fraud, or show that funds are lost. The scenario changes recorded waits under an assumption; feasibility and real-world impact are untested.

    Amounts are denominated in USDC. A recorded credit requires a recorded confirmation in this prototype; reconcile incomplete source records before uploading. At exactly the target, a completed deposit is within target and an unresolved deposit is not yet overdue.
